### [1] [Direct Factorization of the Karhunen-Loève Transform of AR(1) Sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06522)
*Yuriy A. Reznik*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文发现AR(1)源的确切Karhunen–Loève变换(KLT)并非非结构化稠密矩阵，而是可以通过直接递归分解在任意阶数和任意相关系数下实现。分解由蝴蝶级、半阶副本、Cauchy结构的修正级组成，通过快速多极方法可将计算复杂度降至O(N log N)，与FFT同阶。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典的AR(1) KLT长期以来被视为无序稠密矩阵，其频率是Ray和Driver超越方程的根，难以直接计算。因此研究转向快速近似变换（最著名的是DCT）。本文质疑这种退让是否过早，试图重新审视精确KLT是否真的不具备可高效计算的结构。

Method: 论文证明AR(1) KLT在任意阶数N≥2和相关系数ρ∈(0,1)下都允许直接递归分解。偶数阶时，变换化简为蝴蝶级、两个半阶KLT副本，以及由协方差矩阵秩一边界扰动生成的正交修正；奇数阶时，分解为单边预测残差的KLT副本和一个吸收中心样本的arrowhead级。所有修正级均具有Cauchy结构，可用快速多极方法(FMM)以O(N log N)复杂度实现。分解基于Sherman–Morrison恒等式和经典secular特征值更新，当ρ→1时退化为DCT-II的奇偶分裂。

Result: 获得了精确KLT的O(N log N)快速算法，与FFT和快速正弦变换同阶。分解常数被证明是ρ的代数函数，对N≤8可用根式表示；给出了N=2到8的完整精确KLT模块。

Conclusion: 该研究表明此前放弃精确KLT转向近似变换的决策为时过早。AR(1) KLT本身具有丰富的递归结构，可以高效精确计算，且作为ρ→1的极限自然地退化为DCT-II，这重新确立了精确KLT作为变换编码最优基准的地位。

Abstract: The Karhunen--Loève transform (KLT) of the stationary AR(1) source is the classical optimality benchmark of transform coding. Its frequencies are roots of the transcendental equations of Ray and Driver, and the transform has therefore long been treated as an unstructured dense matrix. Rather than compute the exact KLT, the field turned to fast approximations of it, most famously the discrete cosine transform (DCT). This paper shows that the retreat was premature. The exact AR(1) KLT admits direct recursive factorizations, at every order $N\ge2$ and every correlation coefficient $ρ\in(0,1)$: at even orders, the transform reduces to a butterfly stage, two half-order copies of itself, and orthogonal corrections generated by rank-one boundary perturbations of the covariance; at odd orders, to two copies of the KLT of the one-sided prediction residual and an arrowhead stage absorbing the center sample. The residual KLT recurses through half order as well. All correction stages are Cauchy-structured, and applying them by the fast multipole method yields exact-KLT algorithms of $O(N\log N)$ complexity---the same order as the FFT and the fast sinusoidal transforms. The factorizations follow from the covariance matrix by the Sherman--Morrison identity and the classical secular eigenvalue updates, and degenerate, as $ρ\to1$, to the known parity splittings of the DCT-II. As secondary results, the factorization constants are shown to be algebraic functions of $ρ$---in radicals for all $N\le8$---and complete exact KLT modules for $N=2,\dots,8$ are given.
