TL;DR: 本文证明了Shannon提出的关于AWGN信道下等能量信号集最大化正确解码概率问题的严格唯一性结果：任何非正则单纯形的信号集在每个正信噪比下都严格低于正则单纯形界，达到该界即等价于正则单纯形（至多相差顶点重标号和正交变换）。证明基于将Mulgund的论证第一步自卷积用Royen相关定理加强，并用Lean 4机器验证了单参数刚性。

Motivation: Mulgund在2026年证明了正则单纯形值界定了所有信号集在每个信噪比下的正确解码概率，但"正则单纯形是否是唯一最大化器"这一开放问题尚未解决。本文旨在确定等号成立的情形，并以强于唯一性的形式回答此问题。

Method: 作者将Mulgund论证中的第一步自卷积进行加强，借助Royen相关定理进行处理。关键思想是将信号集诱导的相关矩阵与恒等矩阵（独立情形）进行比较：任何非恒等的相关矩阵都会使下尾概率在每一有限阈值处严格高于独立情形。单参数刚性部分通过Lean 4证明助手进行机器验证。

Result: (1) 非正则单纯形的信号集在每个正信噪比下严格低于界；(2) 在一个正工作点达到界的码必为正则单纯形（至多相差顶点重标号和正交映射）；(3) 任何非恒等相关矩阵在下尾概率上严格高于独立对应值；(4) 环境维度低于n的码无法达到界；(5) 在能量预算E和无限制块长下，最优码本唯一为正则单纯形，且每个最优码字耗尽能量配额；(6) Simplex Mean Width猜想等号仅在正则单纯形时成立。

Conclusion: 本文给出的等号情形刻画比唯一性更强，彻底回答了Shannon问题中最大化器的唯一性。结果表明正则单纯形在所有信噪比下且在所有维度设置中都是严格的唯一最优解，并推广到Simplex Mean Width猜想及能量预算下的码书设计问题。

Abstract: Among $n+1$ equiprobable equal-energy signals in $\R^n$ under additive white Gaussian noise with maximum-likelihood decoding, which arrangement maximizes the probability of correct decoding? The question is Shannon's, recorded by Rice in 1950. Mulgund proved in 2026 that the regular-simplex value bounds the correct-decoding probability of every signal set at every signal-to-noise ratio, leaving open whether the simplex is the only maximizer. This paper determines the equality cases in a form stronger than uniqueness. A signal set other than a regular simplex falls strictly below the bound at every positive signal-to-noise ratio. Hence a code meeting the bound at one positive operating point is already a regular simplex, up to vertex relabeling and an orthogonal map. In probabilistic form, among the correlation matrices that signal sets induce, any matrix other than the identity gives a lower-orthant probability strictly above its independent counterpart at every finite threshold, leaving no room for a nontrivial equality. No code of ambient dimension below $n$ attains the bound. Under an energy budget $E$ with unrestricted blocklength the optimal codebook is uniquely the regular simplex of circumradius $\sqrt{E}$. Every optimal codeword therefore exhausts its allowance. Equality in the Simplex Mean Width Conjecture likewise occurs only at the regular simplex. The proof strengthens the first self-convolution step of Mulgund's argument with Royen's correlation theorem. The single-parameter rigidity is machine-checked in Lean 4.
