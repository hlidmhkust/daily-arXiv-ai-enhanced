Motivation: RM码已被证明在多种信道上能达到容量，且投影聚合(PA)译码在实验上表现优异，但这些成就亟需理论支撑，因此需要从理论上分析PA/CPA译码的机制和性能。

Method: 首先证明软判决CPA译码返回精确边际概率且具有对称性；在此基础上建立密度演化模型；为简化模型，用硬判决译码近似投影和快速Hadamard变换译码；最后利用密度演化进行渐近分析。

Result: 密度演化模型能捕捉CPA译码软信息均值和方差的快速下降趋势，定性地解释了译码机制和快速收敛速度；渐近分析表明，对于码率趋于零的RM码，CPA译码可实现误码率趋于零。

Conclusion: CPA译码在理论上具有精确边际概率输出和对称性，所提密度演化模型有效解释了其译码行为，且对于低码率RM码具有渐近最优性能。

Abstract: Reed-Muller (RM) codes have been shown to achieve capacity over a range of channels, and recently proposed projection-aggregation (PA) decoding has been experimentally shown to achieve near-maximum-likelihood decoding performance. These recent achievements motivate theoretical research on PA decoding. In this work, we analyze the density function of the soft output from collapsed projection-aggregation (CPA) decoding for RM codes over the binary-input additive white Gaussian noise (BIAWGN) channel. We prove that soft-decision CPA decoding returns an exact marginal probability and is symmetric. Based on the analysis, we build a density evolution model for CPA decoding. To simplify the density evolution, we approximate the projection and the fast Hadamard transform decoding using hard-decision decoding. Simulation results over the BIAWGN channel show that our proposed density evolution model captures the fast reduction in the mean and the variance of the soft information returned from the CPA decoding, which qualitatively explains the decoding mechanism and the fast convergence speed of the CPA decoding. We perform an asymptotic analysis based on the proposed density evolution, and we show that CPA decoding can achieve a vanishing error probability for RM codes with a vanishing code rate.
